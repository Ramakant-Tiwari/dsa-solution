Chapter 32: Amid Ten Thousand Troops

Deep night, faint wind, melting moon.

The gentle blue-shirted man smiled and said his surname was Chen.

It seemed the entire outline of the National Preceptor’s mansion softened along with him.

The young woman with the bun hairstyle said he was her master.

Rong Yu said nothing. She went straight through the gate, as if leaving them there at the entrance.

The old man, who believed he had long since developed a pair of fiery eyes, became even more certain that this refined Confucian-looking man surnamed Chen was a gatekeeper of the National Preceptor’s mansion.

This person was surely a long-famous expert in the martial world; otherwise, how could he be qualified to guard the National Preceptor’s gate?

Afterward, this gatekeeper surnamed Chen led them inside. They passed a beautiful glazed screen, then another gate, then another screen, and finally entered the first courtyard of the National Preceptor’s mansion. There was a parasol tree. Moonlight fell through the branches and leaves like broken silver across the yard. They did not continue into the second courtyard. Instead, they turned left through a door into a quiet garden with its own world: a small bridge over flowing water, elegant pavilions and terraces, and a pond with lotus leaves. Occasionally, fish flipped their tails and struck the water.

Along the way, it was all casual chatter. For example, he asked the youths why they said studying was useless and told them to explain carefully. For example, he thought studying was useful. The less one was a born scholar, the less one came from wealth and honor, the more one felt studying was a way out. He said nearly half the officials in the National Preceptor’s mansion came from poor families in local prefectures and counties. Only half were child prodigies. The other half, when they first began studying, all thought that if they could become a xiucai or juren in the future, it would already bring glory to their family.

They talked for quite a while. The steady, experienced fish steward Hong Tao kept observing words and expressions. The old man left the chances to speak to the youths.

The National Preceptor’s mansion truly hid dragons and crouching tigers. Just a gatekeeper could be so eloquent and quick-witted. He would be more than qualified as a county magistrate.

Finally, the old man could not help asking, “Lord Chen, may I ask when the National Preceptor will summon us?”

The three youths also came back to their senses. Yes, where was the National Preceptor?

Chen Ping’an looked at the short youth and asked with a smile, “Ma Buhai, I heard you want to learn boxing and open a martial arts hall and escort agency in the future. Do you have a master? If you do not have a suitable candidate yet, I can introduce you to a martial artist. Study under him, and after you finish your training, we can discuss your future.”

Hong Tao understood. Of course, a prime minister’s gatekeeper is a third-rank official. If this man was willing to recommend him, there would be no problem with whom Buhai studied.

Ma Buhai tentatively said, “I want to take the martial arts grandmaster Zheng Qian as my master. Is that possible?”

Chen Ping’an could not help laughing. He kept a straight face and said, “She is one of the four great martial arts grandmasters. Are you sure that if I speak for you, it will work?”

Ma Buhai looked disappointed and had to settle for the next best thing. “Then help me send word to the Four Seas Martial Hall. Buhai and I will take that Hall Master Wei as our master. Rumor has it that in the auxiliary capital Luojing, he once exchanged blows with Grandmaster Zheng. There is some connection. Maybe in the future I can benefit from it and meet Grandmaster Zheng early.”

Hong Tao, however, seemed inspired. He said carefully, “They say it is better to seize the day than choose a distant day. Why not let Buhai take you as his master, Lord Chen? What do you think? Not as a personal disciple; even an unregistered disciple would do. Just treat it as fate meeting in the martial world, and give him a hand.”

Chen Ping’an waved his hand and said with a smile, “Unfortunately, I already have a closed-door disciple. Besides, Ma Buhai’s aptitude for martial arts is a bit lacking. It is not good enough for me to break precedent.”

Hong Tao was speechless. Truly impolite. Worthy of someone making a living in the National Preceptor’s mansion. One word: arrogant.

Ma Buhai was not angry. Instead, he appreciated this fellow’s straightforwardness. A martial world man should not speak in official circles’ roundabout ways.

He cupped his fists and said, “Then Ding Hao, Hu Jin, and I will go study under Hall Master Wei.”

The three brothers had to share hardship and prosperity in this life. As for Old Hong, the three of them would support him in old age.

Chen Ping’an nodded, then turned with a smile to his first disciple. “What do you say? A chance meeting in the martial world, a casual encounter. Give them a hand?”

Pei Qian said helplessly, “Tomorrow I will take them to find Wei Li.”

Chen Ping’an held back a laugh and said, “If you really do not want to, let Guo Zhujiu do it.”

Pei Qian shook her head. “Master, I should go myself. I also want to talk with Wei Li.”

That fellow had thick skin. Back during the interval of the auxiliary capital battle, he had asked to exchange punches with her. He fell in a few punches and earned quite a bit of martial world reputation. That was one thing. She would not even mention the medical fee he had cheated her out of. But after Wei Li came to the capital and opened a martial arts hall, he enshrined that money pouch and offered incense to it every morning before walking the stakes. What was that about?

The tall youth Hu Jin was still thinking about and worrying over that woman whose whereabouts were unknown. Her identity was fake, so her name must also be fake.

Ma Buhai was somewhat puzzled. This young woman named Pei Qian dared to call Hall Master Wei by name?

Ding Hao suddenly said, “Lord Chen, I want to enter Chunshan Academy to study. Is that possible?”

Chen Ping’an asked with a smile, “To become a ‘real official’?”

Ding Hao answered honestly, “I really want to.”

Chen Ping’an asked, “After becoming an official, then what?”

Ding Hao said, “Become a high official.”

Chen Ping’an smiled slightly. “An official must have some aspiration, right? For example, to earn money, for power, or to bring glory to ancestors, to have one’s name written in bold in the clan genealogy, or recorded in the local county annals.”

Ding Hao said, “None of those. I just want to know how the smartest people in the Great Li Dynasty speak and do things.”

Hearing such an answer, Chen Ping’an was clearly surprised. He was silent for a moment, then said, “Then work hard. Once you have an ideal, you must try it.”

Chen Ping’an asked, “What about Hu Jin? Any thoughts? Will you go with Ma Buhai to study martial arts, or with Ding Hao to study at the academy?”

Hu Jin mustered his courage and said, “Lord Chen, may I ask a question?”

Ding Hao was extremely nervous. He hesitated, but did not stop his friend’s rash question. Nor did he care whether tonight they might end up with nothing.

Hong Tao, however, was anxious. He grabbed the tall youth’s arm, his fingers quietly tightening, and said to the blue-shirted man, “Lord Chen, Hu Jin will go to the martial arts hall tomorrow. He will go.”

Hu Jin’s lips moved. In the end, he swallowed the words already at his lips. His eyes dimmed, and he forced a smile. “Lord Chen, tomorrow I will go with Ding Hao to study under a master.”

Then the tall youth cupped his fists and said, “Thank you!”

He hoped that in the martial world in the future, he might meet her again. Could he?

Chen Ping’an said, “Fine, then it is settled.”

He led them back to the first courtyard. Rong Yu came over from the covered corridor and said softly, “Mr. Chen, no need to see them off. I will do it.”

Chen Ping’an nodded. “All right.”

Pei Qian and Rong Yu sent them out of the National Preceptor’s mansion, then returned.

Pei Qian smiled and said, “Master, it seems Ding Hao has already guessed that you are the National Preceptor.”

Chen Ping’an nodded. “He is a very smart youth, and his nature is good.”

Rong Yu silently noted this.

Pei Qian explained, “Master, I did not read their heart-scenes.”

Seeing her master smile without speaking, Pei Qian said anxiously, “Really!”

Rong Yu was somewhat surprised. Was the National Preceptor so strict with Pei Qian? Was the authority of the master so heavy in Pei Qian’s heart?

Only then did Chen Ping’an speak with a smile, “When you were little, I controlled you a lot because I was afraid you would make mistakes. You were young. If you made a mistake, besides admitting it, the consequences in practice would be fixed by your master, right?”

Pei Qian blushed.

Chen Ping’an continued, “After all these years, it has always been you, little black charcoal, earnestly learning. You learned well. The principles went from your ears into your heart. So now it should be Pei Qian who manages the idle matters and wrongs of the world.”

At this moment, Pei Qian finally regained the spirit to walk the martial world again.

Chen Ping’an smiled slightly. “Little donkey, golden leaves, all prepared. This martial world is waiting for Pei Qian to descend the mountain.”

As expected, not long after leaving the National Preceptor’s mansion, Ding Hao told the old man and his two friends his guess.

Pei Qian’s master, Chen Ping’an, was the Great Li National Preceptor. Rong Yu’s line at the end, “she will see the guests off,” was the key. As for her calling him “Mr. Chen,” that was just a cover.

And Pei Qian was the martial arts grandmaster famous throughout the continent, “Zheng Qian.”

Chen Ping’an said, “Before wandering the martial world, remember to ask Senior Chen Yi for advice and spar more.”

Pei Qian nodded.

Rong Yu asked with a smile, “If Ding Hao had hidden his thoughts, how would the National Preceptor view this youth?”

Chen Ping’an said, “Then he would stop at ‘smart.’ What I do is prevent the Great Li Dynasty from being destroyed by smart people, and prevent cleverness and cunning from casually toying with, insulting, and killing the honest and kind. These youths have good natures. Rong Yu, the National Preceptor’s mansion should pay more attention.”

Rong Yu understood clearly. Tomorrow the National Preceptor would separately meet two groups of the most powerful smart people in the Great Li Dynasty. They… would be blessed.

Song Yunjian still stood under the peach tree, counting the peach blossoms, enjoying it endlessly.

Lin Shouyi and Cao Qinglang took advantage of the bright moonlight to play Go in the second courtyard.

The cook Yu Qing hesitated, then went to the door and asked if they wanted a midnight snack.

In the neighboring courtyard, the room of the ancient shaman still gave off a faint yellow light. Reading under the lamp, it looked like he would stay up all night.

Zhu Su finished refining qi and came out. She leaned against the corridor pillar, looking at the map of the Wildlands situation in the courtyard.

Rong Yu asked, “National Preceptor, how should I reply to His Majesty?”

It turned out the National Preceptor’s mansion had specially opened a Duobao Pavilion, a three-story building under an illusion.

Earlier, Chen Ping’an had Rong Yu draw up a list for the emperor, originally intended to store and keep these treasures.

But after the Three Courts Dharma Master pulled this move, Chen Ping’an did not really want to “use public office for private gain.”

However, His Majesty’s words were also interesting. He had already arranged for people to handle it, so there was no reason for them to work in vain.

Rong Yu said, “His Majesty’s meaning is simple. For cultivators, heavenly materials and earthly treasures are never too many. The thicker the foundation, the better. As long as it can help the National Preceptor improve his Dao power, those secret warehouses of Great Li used to store various magic treasures and spiritual tools are not the Ministry of Revenue’s treasury. Even if emptied, it does not matter.”

Chen Ping’an thought for a moment and said, “Then bring them all over.”

“Pei Qian, call Cao Qinglang. You two go with Rong Yu to the secret warehouse to select treasures.”

“Bring Yu Shiwu, Xu Jiaoqie, and the others too. And Yu Qing. Allow them to each choose one treasure outside the list.”

Noisy and bustling, they went to get rich. Yu Qing wanted to refuse, but Rong Yu’s mind and words were so skillful that she easily persuaded this cook who had given up returning to the Cherry Green Robe lineage.

Only Lin Shouyi was not suitable to take a treasure.

Chen Ping’an took his student Cao Qinglang’s seat and played Go with Lin Shouyi.

Originally the game was even, but Chen Ping’an placed stones like flying. The more Lin Shouyi played, the slower he became. The situation increasingly favored Chen Ping’an. When Lin Shouyi once again picked up a black stone from the bamboo Go bowl and fell into thought.

Chen Ping’an said with a smile, “Lin Yupu, finally know who the real bad Go player is?”

There is only the rule that a spectator should keep silent; there is no rule that the player cannot speak. When it was his turn, attacking the heart was best.

Lin Shouyi hesitantly placed a stone and asked in confusion, “Your Go has improved so much? How did you do it?”

Chen Ping’an picked up a white stone and said seriously, “It looks like ghosts hide under my wrist, as if aided by gods. In truth, it is because I was always talented and have long accumulated skill. Before, I deliberately hid my ability so you bad players would not lose interest in playing.”

When Chen Ping’an placed another stone, Lin Shouyi resigned and silently looked at the board. Chen Ping’an’s Go strength was indeed far above his and Cao Qinglang’s.

Lin Shouyi asked curiously, “Can you beat Cui Dongshan now?”

Chen Ping’an immediately broke character. “That still will not do. I still need handicap stones.”

Lin Shouyi keenly noticed that Chen Ping’an seemed to have changed recently. The watershed was that Heaven-and-Earth Communication.

Chen Ping’an sent a secret voice transmission: “The earlier Chen Ping’an was of course still Chen Ping’an. I am myself. I am me. But divinity and humanity had their primary and secondary reversed. So the earlier Chen Ping’an, with divinity in charge, had all emotions arranged properly. In achievements, my every thought, word, and deed pursued and imitated Senior Brother Cui’s realm. The human self that divinity cut away, dismantled, and forgot, the human self imprisoned, had to obediently endure it. Like… a caged sparrow.”

Chen Ping’an reached out and gently covered the Go bowl. “After the Heaven-and-Earth Communication ended, the primary and secondary reversed again. Humanity became the master. Those suppressed emotions did not disappear. It was like the human heart’s heaven and earth simultaneously suffered a broken dam and a reverse tide.”

How dangerous was such a mental state? Lin Shouyi felt a chill down his spine and asked, “And your Dao heart did not collapse?”

Chen Ping’an smiled. “It happened that I went to Youyi Peak to drink at Liu Xianyang and She Yue’s wedding feast. That day was also the fifth of the fifth month. It untied one of the greatest knots of my life. Of course I was especially happy. When a person’s heart opens, he will not get stuck in a dead end.”

“Next, I fought the ancient shaman. That was also satisfying.”

“Especially afterward, when I went to sea with Cao Ci to exchange punches. It was even more exhilarating. Placed between sea and sky, far from land, my mental state broadened.”

“And tonight’s casual chat is also a necessary ‘relaxation.’ Cultivating body and nature is like governing water. Blocking is worse than guiding. That is why the old temple master said I finally understood a bit of the skill of ‘nourishing the spirit.’”

Only after hearing this did Lin Shouyi stop suspecting Chen Ping’an was seemingly calm but actually mad.

He smiled and said, “Those youths seem a bit like Liu, Chen, and Gu from our hometown back then.”

Chen Ping’an said softly, “If they can hold hope and look up at tomorrow, then we can also calmly look back at yesterday.”

Lin Shouyi nodded deeply. After a moment of silence, he asked, “Another game?”

Chen Ping’an was already cleaning up the stones. He clicked his tongue. “Learning from me and Cao Ci exchanging punches, only losing feels good?”

Lin Shouyi suddenly asked, “One knot is untied. Are there a second and third?”

Chen Ping’an said, “Of course.”

Lin Shouyi asked, “For example? And when will you act?”

Chen Ping’an teased, “Lin Yupu, do not get distracted. Focus on the imperial examination. Earn your jinshi title. Then you can hear fewer complaints from Uncle Lin.”

Lin Shouyi’s face darkened. He carried the two Go bowls back to his room.

Chen Ping’an walked alone to the neighboring second courtyard and looked at the map of the Haoran and Wildlands armies facing each other.

For example, returning to the battlefield and greatly slaughtering the Wildlands.

For example, later asking the sword of White Jade Capital.

Zhu Su leaned on the railing, also looking at the map. Because she was a private sword, she had stayed deep in the Wildlands for a long time, so she had also added some landscape. She was about to speak when she found the Hidden Official had already summoned the Three Mountains Talisman. It was not one he had copied himself, nor one drawn by Bai Jing, but an authentic Three Mountains Talisman. She did not know where he was going.

Soon Zhu Su knew the Hidden Official’s whereabouts.

On the central axis, the first courtyard had the Haoran situation map, the second had the Wildlands map, and the third had the mountains and rivers of Baoping Continent.

The first mountain the Hidden Official appeared at was the relatively close Piyun Mountain in Baoping Continent.

Only because on the “map” laid out in the sky well of the last courtyard, Piyun Mountain was not especially prominent, but at this moment an unusual “ripple of aura” appeared. Being able to directly cause an anomaly on a continent’s map, one could imagine how great the disturbance at Piyun Mountain was.

The second mountain crossed continents directly to Sui Mountain in Middle Earth Divine Continent. Sui Mountain also had the reputation and honor of being the first sacred mountain of Haoran.

Only because in the first courtyard, there was a “faint” sound of drumbeats.

Zhu Su moved there, her gaze quickly swimming over the map. Where was the third mountain? That would be the destination of tonight’s impromptu long journey by the Hidden Official.

A moment later, Zhu Su turned her head in surprise and looked back at the original courtyard. The destination was in the Wildlands!

In the Azure Profound World, a bright white moon hung in the sky. At the entrance of the Daoist temple, the “door god” holding an iron mace, Gu He, was watching the Bi Xiao Cave Master hang a plaque.

The thin bamboo pole Wang Yuanlu had already followed Ya Xiang Yao Qing to the human world. Gu He felt the already cold and quiet Dao field was even lonelier.

The old temple master stepped back a few steps, hands behind his back, looked up at the plaque, and asked, “How is it?”

Xun Lanling, who had been the fire-tending boy at the temple for many years, tactfully echoed, “Good calligraphy. Full of strength, able to match heaven and earth.”

Gu He asked in confusion, “Temple Master, whose work is it?”

The Bi Xiao Cave Master was proud and arrogant, with seniority and Dao power. Since he was willing to go out and ask someone to write it, the person must be a strong fourteenth realm of similar status and on good terms?

The old temple master said, “It is that young sword cultivator you think is of the same kind as me, Chen Ping’an.”

Gu He was stunned. He had always planned that if he went out traveling in the future and saw that surnamed Chen, he would take a detour and avoid his edge. In the end, he would have to stare at him every day?

Xun Lanling wished he could pick up the words he had just said and chew them back into his stomach.

The old temple master said, “Xun Lanling, quietly make a trip to the human world. Use your life-bound secret technique to protect Wang Yuanlu and keep him from unexpected disaster.”

Xun Lanling was clearly unwilling.

The old temple master said, “If Wang Yuanlu dies outside, you do not need to return to the temple to continue tending the fire and refining pills. You may roam freely and open a mountain and establish a sect in some prefecture, becoming its founding ancestor. Remember not to gild the lily by hanging a portrait of me in the ancestral hall or secret room, or erecting a spirit tablet with my Dao name. Do not reveal any connection between you and the Guandao Temple. Otherwise, I will make another trip and personally clean up the sect.”

Xun Lanling immediately panicked. His expression was bleak. He knelt and would not rise, choking, “Has this disciple done something to displease Master, to deserve such severe punishment?”

The old temple master said calmly, “Not knowing the human Dao, one cannot see the immortal Dao. Not understanding the human heart, one cannot communicate with Heaven. You have tended the fire in the temple for many years, but you are still far from ready. Staying by my side, you will only pretend to read Daoist books and secret texts every day and make no progress. Go down the mountain quickly. Do not chatter.”

Xun Lanling rose in sorrow. He did not dare entangle himself in this matter with his master. He returned to his room, packed his bundle and soft goods, and went to toil in the human world.

The old temple master instructed, “Besides protecting Wang Yuanlu, when you travel the human world, you may only experience the red dust as a cultivator of the lower five realms. At most, you may use one life-bound item of magic treasure rank. If you violate this, Gu He will find you, and then you will know you have been expelled from the temple.”

As he spoke, the old temple master waved his fly whisk and tossed a bundle from inside the temple to the fire-tending boy’s feet.

Xun Lanling immediately prostrated again and kowtowed nine times to bid farewell to his master.

After the boy rose, he slung the bundle over his shoulder. Reluctant to leave, he looked back at every step. Seeing his master already step over the threshold into the temple, the boy’s heart grieved. He had to collect his emotions. In his heart, he said to that tall back, “Master, take care of your health. This disciple is going out to travel.” The boy turned his head, raised his arm to wipe his face, and rode the wind away from the bright moon, heading toward the Sui Chu Palace.

Gu He sighed endlessly. The Bi Xiao Cave Master was too cruel… Then he heard the Bi Xiao Cave Master shout angrily from the alchemy room door, “Are you standing there stupidly for what?”

Gu He felt lost in clouds. Whether I serve the Dao field as a mountain-supplying offering or serve the temple as a door god, if I do not stand in place, should I wander around like the day and night wandering gods of a city god temple?

The old temple master had to curse this blockhead, “Idiot, still not rolling off to be Xun Lanling’s hidden Dao protector?”

Gu He was overjoyed. He had long felt he owed Daoist Xun too much. Protecting his Dao for this life and body was only right.

Gu He immediately formed a Daoist seal, concealed his form, and was about to quietly follow Xun Lanling. Suddenly his heart shook. His Dao body and Dao heart sank into mud, and he could not move. He heard the Bi Xiao Cave Master give an unfriendly “Hm.” Gu He immediately understood. He turned, faced his temple master, and properly bowed farewell. Sure enough, only then did his Dao techniques circulate without obstruction.

Then he heard the Bi Xiao Cave Master instruct, “When you reach the land, do not use borrowed authority to bully others. Otherwise, if Heaven does not punish you, I will! But also remember this: in the future, if you meet someone on the Dao who lacks eyes, help them open their eyes. Do not be vague. When striking, do not shrink back!”

Gu He was delighted. He happily accepted this Dao decree, hid his tracks, and went to chase the boy who was “a friend of old years, now a fellow disciple.”

No matter how long his Dao age, the fire-tending boy Xun Lanling was still young at heart. Halfway there, he stopped in a sea of clouds and cried loudly.

Gu He hid at the edge of the cloud sea, his heart also sorrowful. In the end, Gu He restrained himself and did not appear.

Earlier, when the attention of both worlds was on the Bi Xiao Cave Master and the Three Courts Dharma Master fighting.

An old Daoist had voluntarily appeared in the territory of the Sui Chu Palace. He sent a heartfelt message to the stork tower towering by the river, “This poor Daoist is Zhang Jiao, Dao name Huang Tian. I seek an audience with Palace Master Wu. I have a matter to discuss.”

This Daoist was an old fourteenth realm. He had not gained power in the Azure Profound World, so he had gone to the Western Buddha Kingdom.

Seeing his homeland world, now in turmoil, with only three of the fourteen Azure Profound prefectures still neutral and not entangled in the confrontation between White Jade Capital and the Sui Chu Palace, the old Daoist, though well aware this was his opportunity to rise, still felt gloomy.

Zhang Jiao looked at the anomaly overhead, squinted, stroked his beard, and thought to himself, “I wonder which side Runyue Peak will favor?”

Actually, he worried that all cultivators of the upper five realms in the world could see this scene.

It was a clash between two pseudo-fifteenth-realm cultivators, hard against hard, with no fancy tricks.

Zhang Jiao turned to White Jade Capital. Finally, its “true face” had appeared: a Jade Capital Mountain formed when the Dao Ancestor personally gathered earth as its foundation!

Thus for ten thousand years, the five cities and twelve towers of White Jade Capital seemed suspended in the air, their positions differing in height.

To this day, except for the Great Master’s Cyan City and Third Master Lu Chen’s Nanhua City—because the two masters, for their own reasons, were temporarily absent—the other three cities and twelve towers were all now within the mountain.

From the peak of Jade Capital Mountain, where Sect Master Yu Dou was, to the Dharma form of Yao Qing, a spectacular “rainbow bridge” spanning half the world appeared.

Yu Dou wore a feathered robe, held a long sword, and a sword light pressed straight toward Yao Qing’s Dharma form above the Sui Chu Palace.

Yao Qing’s Dharma form held a long spear, its tip pointed at White Jade Capital, also stirring up a vast Dao true intent that manifested as a crimson flood of military momentum, opposing Yu Dou’s sword light.

Sword light and weapon aura collided, forming that long rainbow.

Every moment, both sides consumed Dao power. Judging by the length of the two sections of rainbow, Yu Dou’s side was clearly far superior.

But on the lands of more than ten Azure Profound prefectures, wherever smoke rose, wisps of green smoke would actively merge into Yao Qing’s military manifestation, rising as green smoke. Occasionally there were specks of gold and silver, presumably the fall of local mountain-and-water gods, Daoist officials, and cultivators.

Sword light hung in the sky, with the awe-inspiring spirit to shake heaven and earth and suppress the whole world.

The spear pointed, with the ambition to overturn heaven and earth and pierce through White Jade Capital.

Zhang Jiao had just arrived in the Azure Profound World. He deduced a little, quickly hit a wall, and did not continue. After all, once it stirred Yao Qing’s Dao heart or caused Yao Qing’s disgust and hatred, Zhang Jiao could easily suffer inexplicable calamities, even immediate backlash from Dao techniques. Yao Qing was a pseudo-fifteenth realm and already had the embryonic form of Dao heart as Heavenly heart.

Moreover, if his probing distracted Yao Qing, would it not help the White Jade Capital Yu Dou and those Daoist officials who had their eyes on their foreheads?

Zhang Jiao could not understand why Yao Qing had to do this. The three-time chief minister of the Qing Shen Dynasty, a fourteenth realm by his own ability, why take this path with no retreat?

The old Daoist collected his thoughts. Although he had come to form an alliance, he still did not dare be careless. He was as if facing a great enemy.

Only because Wu Shuangjiang came personally. Even Daoist Zhang Jiao did not dare say this Palace Master Wu would definitely agree to him.

On the path of cultivation, the young are formidable.

After all, Wu Shuangjiang had earlier proclaimed to the world that joint execution. His two allies were one who seemed to want to establish a religion and become an ancestor in the Wildlands, Zheng Juzhong, and one who, by himself, brought about the Heaven-and-Earth Communication, Chen Ping’an.

Deep in the Wildlands.

A high mountain that had recently changed its surname to “Haoran.” The original sect-level Dao field buildings and several old shrines had all become the meeting place for the generals and commanders of several Haoran dynasties.

This army had advanced too quickly on the battlefield, appearing greedy for merit and advancing alone.

Although the original intention was to lure the enemy and then strike reinforcements, they had been too victorious. Moreover, the great demons of the Wildlands had joined forces using a great divine ability similar to opening a passage to the Abyss. The Wildlands co-lord Fei Ran had several new thrones mobilized. In the confrontation, the balance of forces changed unexpectedly, and the Wildlands numbers swelled.

The Wildlands military tent’s tactics were extremely crude. They wanted to decide the war in one battle, to swallow this army in one bite, and then be surrounded by Haoran in turn. The Wildlands demons left on the battlefield who could not withdraw would all die in battle. Thus the Wildlands did not care about battle losses or consequences. They only needed a “great victory” to boost morale.

On Haoran’s side, the main force was the thirty thousand elite cavalry of the Chengguan Dynasty. The remaining seven hundred thousand were steadily approaching from the rear. According to the agreed deadline, the main force behind would arrive in three days. Even if the accompanying great cultivators used divine abilities and accelerated various ferries, they could only shorten it to two days. Otherwise, it would truly become “adding oil” again and again.

At the mountain top, a young man in plain clothes watched the battlefield outside the mountain expressionlessly. The Wildlands demons had already sounded the horn for a major attack.

Even though they were hundreds of miles apart, the dense mass of demons was like swarming ants, like a plague of locusts. At both ends of the battlefield, countless spells had already lit up, each smashing toward the other. At the same time, both sides had great formations protecting their army formations. Compared to Haoran’s neat order, the demon side was extremely savage. Just the thousands of catapults threw “boulders,” among which were entire illegal shrines that had been uprooted and moved here, “snowballs” of hundreds of white-bone skeletons mixed together, and a “pond” condensed from the water of the Yeluo River. When it landed, it would explode, scattering like a hundred thousand arrows.

Beside the young man watching from the mountain top, besides the commanders of several dynasties and his personal guards, there were two armored generals. They were not only a martial arts grandmaster and a great cultivator, but also battle-hardened commanders skilled at fighting “dull battles.”

The young man said, “After the spells wear down the formations, you will need to lead the army to pierce through the enemy formation at least twice.”

One commander’s eyes burned. He smiled and said, “Twice would not show the prowess of our Chengguan iron cavalry. At least three times.”

At least after the third cavalry penetration, whether they could return did not matter.

The young man nodded and said, “Go.”

The two commanders directly flipped over the railing and quickly rode the wind down to the formation at the mountain foot. They did not say any heroic words to the young man, nor any farewell.
