**Chapter 38: Hidden Official Meets Hidden Official**

Deep in the night, while Song Yunjian was strolling in the National Preceptor’s mansion, he ran into Rong Yu, who was equally uneasy. They simply stood together and looked at the map of the Wildlands’ mountains and rivers in the courtyard, silently waiting for the National Preceptor to return. Looking up, one could see a newly built Duobao Tower, carved beams and painted rafters, like a lady under the moon in a gold-and-green landscape scroll. At the top floor, treasure light overflowed and seeped through the window lattice. There were piled the ancient ritual vessels Chen Ping’an had obtained from the great shaman, simple in shape, with a coarse texture, far less exquisite than the magic treasures of later ages.

They chatted idly about capital gossip, but were somewhat distracted. A country cannot be without a ruler for a day, and the Great Li Dynasty could not lack its National Preceptor to hold the seat.

Earlier, a bright moon had suddenly risen out of nowhere. Cultivators could all sense the mighty Dao aura rippling through Haoran. Although the moon’s outline quickly faded, such an anomaly still made those with minds full of doubt and alarm wonder what great event was about to happen in the world.

Song Yunjian suddenly said, “He’s back.”

Across the covered walkway, besides the martial sisters Pei Qian and Guo Zhujiu, there were also the female sword immortal Zhu Su, and Zhou Haijing and the other twelve Earthly Branches. All had appeared.

Seeing such a noisy crowd, Song Yunjian felt relieved, but he did not see the National Preceptor. Anxiously he asked, “Where is the National Preceptor?”

Xie Gou grinned and said, “The little Master waved his hand, and we were all thrown back. The Mountain Master needs to go see Lu Chen. Before parting, he told us not to worry.”

Pei Qian nodded and said, “Master said he definitely will not miss tomorrow’s morning court.”

Xie Gou looked up at the top of Duobao Tower, then turned to Rong Yu, rubbed her hands ingratiatingly, and said, “Sister Rong Yu, I want to enter Duobao Tower. This trip was not wasted. I finally learned the true uses of those treasures. Do I need to register at the door, and be searched on the way out to prevent private smuggling?”

Rong Yu smiled and said, “This building is the National Preceptor’s private property. Miss Xie is the chief offering, so naturally there is no need to record it.”

Xie Gou waved her hand and laughed, “What chief offering? It is all the Mountain Master’s trust and my colleagues’ kindness.”

Even though Yuan Huajing and the others had already left that battlefield, they were still deeply stirred.

Han Zhoujin could not help sighing, “Almost had to see countless lives and deaths again.”

The little monk pressed his palms together and silently recited a Buddha’s name.

Lu Hui said, “The Chengguan iron cavalry is indeed not weak.”

Zhou Haijing asked with a smile, “That handsome young man is the Chengguan Emperor?”

Gai Yan rolled her eyes. This woman always looked like she wanted to marry an emperor.

Song Xu nodded and said, “Definitely. The two divine anomalies beside him are clearly the literary fortune and martial fortune of the Chengguan Dynasty manifested.”

Yuan Huajing asked, “That water-stopping mirror?”

Ku Shou said, “Not a big problem.”

Xie Gou entered the top floor and stared at those ancient sacrificial objects. Since they could bear merit and virtue, they could wrestle with the river of time. Old things used in new ways, wonderful beyond words.

The next batch of mountain-and-river ferries of the Great Li Dynasty would be truly impressive.

The girl in the sable hat put her hands on her hips. “Ha, what the bandits can do, I can do too.”

Why not ask the Mountain Master for a temporary ferry construction inspector post? Skills do not burden the body, and neither do official hats. The more the better.

On a desolate Gobi desert, a thin man wearing a bamboo hat and a bamboo sword at his waist shaded his eyes with his hand. The earth was like a table, and a red sun hanging in the sky and a long crimson mountain range stretched across like a screen of a hundred treasures inlaid inkstone. The man looked into the distance and vaguely saw a few wisps of cooking smoke. He adjusted his hat, glanced sideways at a certain place, and continued on his way.

Soon, from that direction, an old man in a yellow robe with an ethereal immortal air walked out. He saluted from afar and asked with a smile in pure Haoran elegant speech, “Daoist Chen, would you mind traveling together for a while?”

The bamboo-hatted man said nothing and did not stop. He only reached out and pressed the sword hilt at his waist.

Travel together for a while? That was indeed possible. But after a while, they would likely part ways, one to yin and one to yang.

The old Daoist’s eyelids twitched slightly. Keeping some distance, he walked on the Gobi desert, the sand crunching underfoot, and said directly, “Daoist Chen, on our Wildlands side, besides Daoist Bai Ze, Fei Ran and Gui Ke, that Daoist couple, will also participate in the discussion. On your Haoran side, there are the Ritual Sage and Liu Xiang.”

Chen Ping’an asked in Wildlands elegant speech, “How did you find my trail?”

Master Yan smiled and said, “To be honest, purely by luck.”

Chen Ping’an said, “Then your luck is not very good.”

Earlier, Master Yan had voluntarily placed himself on the battlefield, making him the second great Wildlands figure after Bai Ze to declare he would enter the arena. He looked like an unquestionable advocate of war.

Master Yan laughed heartily, “I think my luck is quite good. It is an auspicious day.”

If they could reach an agreement, each would get what they needed. You should hand over your sword; I should offer my head.

Unfortunately, there was no longer any ruins of Sword Qi Great Wall in the world. The matter of sword cultivators carving words after killing great demons had naturally become an old calendar.

Chen Ping’an said, “To travel ten thousand miles just to deliver your head, one could call it deep affection and heavy loyalty. If all Wildlands demons were as reasonable as you, that would be good.”

The old temple master had indeed mentioned the matter of sword-liberating Master Yan. But Master Yan delivering himself to the door still felt somewhat wrong. A cautious sailor can navigate ten thousand years, especially in the Wildlands.

Master Yan was momentarily speechless. Seeing that the Hidden Official had no intention of speaking, he could only make conversation: “Before coming here, I was extremely puzzled. I could not believe there really was a heroic figure as selfless as you, Daoist. Fortunately, an expert leaked a heavenly secret and cleared my confusion. He said a god who cannot be sought wrongly has a strong self-destructive tendency, just like ordinary people tirelessly pursuing long life and lasting vision.”

Chen Ping’an nodded and said, “Whether a certain existence’s ‘self’ is too thin, or the ‘self’ is too tough, neither is good.”

Master Yan stroked his beard and smiled, “Those who prove the Dao through force are like splitting a mountain with an axe. Those whose mysterious words are empty are like drawing water with a bamboo basket.”

Chen Ping’an said, “Not like something a Wildlands powerhouse would say.”

Master Yan said with emotion for no apparent reason, “It seems life has many, many scenic spots that one passes by and misses.”

Chen Ping’an raised his hand and pinched his bamboo hat, saying, “Although we cannot decide what we see, we can decide what we remember.”

Master Yan nodded in agreement. In the Wildlands, the old man had not spoken such extra words with a Daoist friend for a long time.

His disciples and grand-disciples stared at their “ancestor.” Cultivators outside the mountain tried every means to come to his door and beg for all kinds of strange “talismans.” The whole Wildlands world was watching when his “Ascension” would become “Fourteenth.”

He remembered the last time they discussed the Dao was probably when that Wenhai Zhoumi, who tried to recreate the Wildlands, visited the Yufu Palace.

All the elegance of ancient times had been blown away by wind and rain.

Chen Ping’an casually asked, “May I ask, Senior, in recent years in the Wildlands, have there been any stunningly talented young cultivators who are temporarily unknown but destined to gain great fame in the future?”

Master Yan smiled ambiguously.

Was this young Hidden Official, who worried about the survival of the world, asking whether there were Wildlands prodigies who would rise in response to the calamity? Did he want to “find them by following the map” and kill them early, lest they quietly grow into great threats?

Master Yan naturally would not answer. He only asked curiously, “Daoist Chen, you walk alone under heaven. What are you seeking?”

The words had a double meaning.

Chen Ping’an said, “Fishing. Big or small does not matter. I cannot return empty-handed.”

Master Yan stopped and smiled, “Have I not taken the bait? As for whether you can drag me ashore, I fear it depends on the Hidden Official’s Dao power.”

Chen Ping’an also stopped. Master Yan stopped with him. They turned sideways and looked at each other. It seemed there was no longer any need to mention the old temple master.

Coming here through a secret art to see the Hidden Official, the old man wanted to confirm one thing. He had long heard that Chen Ping’an was quite close to the Guandao Temple in the East Sea, and now with Bai Jing aiding him, he must indeed have a deep relationship with the Bi Xiao Cave Master.

As for seeking liberation, it did not mean stretching out one’s neck for a sword cultivator to chop off one’s head.

For a Daoist who had attained the Dao, successfully achieving weapon liberation was not such a simple good thing.

The higher the realm, the more troublesome. Therefore dying in the arena was the cleanest ending.

The old Daoist had a vague premonition. If he died gloriously in battle, especially if he could be killed by Qi Tingji, then besides gaining a sword liberation, he might even start another Dao fate.

All that came before is like death yesterday; all that comes after is like birth today.

The old man looked at the sky. In the furnace of creation, suffering heaven’s tempering, bitter indeed. In the puppet theater, competing for victory and defeat, sad indeed.

Actually, what Chen Ping’an was really waiting for was Zou Zi’s uninvited arrival, or the roadside waiting that Zou Zi excelled at.

No matter. Big fish or small fish, all are catches.

Just as both sides were about to fight, on the road ahead, a very conspicuous figure appeared, swaying.

Like a spot of ink. Soon she came closer. She was a little girl with ram-horn braids, wearing a black robe. She was twisting her wrists, her eyes burning.

Two generations of Hidden Officials met.

Master Yan smiled bitterly and explained to Chen Ping’an, “Believe it or not, this has nothing to do with me.”

Xiao Xun did not care at all about this leader of Wildlands talisman Dao. She just stared straight at Chen Ping’an and asked, “Is Haoran really that good?”

In other words, is it worth you risking your life like this?

Chen Ping’an narrowed his eyes and said, “Maybe not that good, but definitely better than you imagine.”

Xiao Xun grinned and said, “Do you hate me? Of course you hate me. I am just curious how much.”

For public and private reasons, there would be resentment. She betrayed Sword Qi Great Wall and ambushed her senior brother Zuo You on the battlefield.

Chen Ping’an said calmly, “It cannot be called irreconcilable hatred, but it is enough that when I see you, I will not let you ‘slip by.’”

Xiao Xun tilted her head, confused. “What does that mean?”

Chen Ping’an could not be bothered to explain.

Master Yan said with a smile, “If I have not misunderstood, Hidden Official Chen means he wants to fight Daoist Xiao Xun to the death.”

Xiao Xun said, “Oh.” She gave the old Daoist a thumbs-up. “Old Yunshen, your learning is enormous. Admirable, admirable.”

Then she slowly turned the thumb downward.

“Master Yan” was instantly blown apart by a punch from her.

Unfortunately, it was only a substitute talisman that could pass as real.

Xiao Xun curled her lip, swung her arms a few times, and mocked, “An old undying thing who plays ghosts and gods.”

Far away, on the ridge of that crimson mountain range, Shou Chen, carrying a sword case, and his junior sister Liu Bai, wearing a veil, stood together looking toward the yellow sand road.

In the past, at Sword Qi Great Wall, “Shou Chen in the south, Hidden Official in the north” was a saying of great weight.

Like “White-clothed Cao, blue-shirted Chen” in Haoran’s martial Dao.

Both were titles and reputations hard fought and earned.

The next moment, Xiao Xun felt a chill down her spine. Shou Chen and Liu Bai saw a blue-shirted figure flutter. He had already assumed a fist stance, aimed straight at the back of her head.

Because of the great difference in height, he could not punch through Xiao Xun’s back and heart. From the stance, it looked like he was going to chop off her entire head with one punch.

Liu Bai’s cultivation was weaker. Her eyes immediately seeped blood, forcing her to turn her head away. Shou Chen’s Dao body was strong, so he could continue watching.

Xiao Xun did not dodge or avoid. Instead of temporarily evading the edge, she leaned her head back hard and took the punch with her head.

In an instant, yellow sand filled the sky, dust flew. Vaguely, a huge creature seemed to crouch on the earth.

When the dust settled, Xiao Xun returned to her human form. She reached around to the back of her head. Besides blood, there was brain matter.

The ground behind Xiao Xun looked like a great tree had fallen, with countless branches.

From this, one could see the force of that punch.

Liu Bai looked at the battlefield again, shocked.

Shou Chen’s expression was grave. Before coming, Xiao Xun had told them not to meddle, or she would turn on them.

If Zheng Juzhong had not instructed the two of them to keep an eye on Xiao Xun, he would not have wanted to wade into these muddy waters.

Xiao Xun seemed oblivious. She flicked her hand, splattering blood on the ground. “You did not expect it, did you? The sword cultivator who killed the most demons at Sword Qi Great Wall back then was actually a demon.”

Chen Ping’an nodded and said, “I really did not expect it.”

Xiao Xun said, “Today I just came to greet you face to face. I will stay in the Wildlands waiting for you to seek revenge.”

Chen Ping’an said, “We only know that the sword cultivator who killed the most demons in the history of Sword Qi Great Wall was called Xiao Xun.”

He adjusted the bamboo-woven hat. Before leaving the Wildlands with a talisman pinched between two fingers, Chen Ping’an said, “Next time we meet, it will be to kill a demon.”

Xiao Xun stood alone in place.

She raised her hand and tugged her ram-horn braids. The weight of the word “we” seemed heavier than that solid punch.

In the many palaces, Emperor Song He was still in the imperial study, truly unable to sleep. He repeatedly twirled between thumb and forefinger a jade-handled brush specially used for writing small elegant script.

This was a trick the emperor had learned from his daughter Song Lian. Back then, the princess had been lectured several times by the old tutors, but repeatedly refused to change. The old tutors complained to the emperor. Song He immediately scolded his daughter severely, and only then did Song Lian change the habit. But privately, Song He himself secretly learned it. Of course, he would not do such a thing during small court meetings.

Tomorrow he would set out for North Ju Lu Continent. The alliance of three dynasties was not enough to keep the Great Li Emperor awake with nervousness. It was more because this was Song He’s first true long journey, and he found it very fresh. After all, the farthest he had gone before was the countryside of Yunzhou, to invite Mr. Chen to serve as National Preceptor. This time it was a real crossing of seas and continents.

The chief eunuch of the Directorate of Ceremonial entered the imperial study and said softly, “Your Majesty, the National Preceptor’s mansion has already taken the treasures. Rong Yu led the group. It was quite lively.”

Song He placed the brush on a celadon brush rest, put both hands behind his head, leaned back in the chair, and said with a smile, “That is good.”

As for the archival documents sent from the National Preceptor’s mansion to the imperial palace, Song He never looked at them. He only had Zhang Yuan carefully store them unchanged. Song He planned to leave them for the future emperor of Great Li, so that he, or… she, would know what the National Preceptor, or the former National Preceptor Chen Ping’an, had done for the Great Li Song clan, and how much effort he had spent.

As the number one eunuch of the Great Li court, Zhang Yuan said with worry, “The National Preceptor went to the Wildlands on a whim. Martial Master Pei, Sword Immortal Zhu Su, and the others have all returned. Only the National Preceptor has not yet returned.”

Song He said, “I trust the National Preceptor has his own considerations.”

Emperor Song He was not a cultivator, but even he understood what a fourteenth-realm Great Li National Preceptor meant. Yet National Preceptor Chen was not anxious at all.

Song He rubbed his temples and said helplessly, “In this world, there is only the saying ‘the emperor is not anxious, but the eunuch is.’ How can there be a saying ‘the National Preceptor is not anxious, but the emperor is’?”

Song He glanced at Zhang Yuan. “Do not mind.”

Zhang Yuan smiled and said, “If Your Majesty says do not mind, then I must mind.”

Song He said softly, “All these years, you have worked hard.”

Zhang Yuan shook his head. In his life he had served three Song emperors. He had seen so many heroes, sages, gentlemen, and extraordinary people. He did not need to risk his life in battle, nor did he need to rack his brains to devise strategies. He only needed to handle the matters before him and at hand. What was hard about that?

Song He hesitated, then asked softly, “Can Song Geng really not be a good emperor?”

The chief eunuch said nothing.

Song He said, “Just treat it as friends chatting about family matters, Zhang Yuan. There are no outsiders here.”

The old man still shook his head.

This was one of the iron rules National Preceptor Cui had set for the eunuchs back then. Violators died.

Even though National Preceptor Cui was no longer in Great Li, his rules remained.

Song He asked, “What about Song Xu, if he gave up cultivation?”

Zhang Yuan remained silent.

Song He said with a bitter face, “A father knows his daughter best. Do I not know Song Lian’s temper?”

“It is already very difficult for a man to be emperor, let alone a woman, and even more so the emperor of Great Li.”

“Just thinking about it makes my heart ache.”

“But if Rong Yu can be Great Li’s National Preceptor, then it seems Song Lian inheriting the throne is not impossible?”

“Then how should her marriage be counted in the future? Would it be marrying out or… never mind, never mind. The boat will straighten when it reaches the bridge.”

Zhang Yuan only listened silently to the emperor’s soliloquy.

Song He gathered his thoughts. It seemed he remembered something very interesting and could not help laughing. “Still, I must urge him again. I insist the National Preceptor agree to serve as chief examiner for this imperial examination.”

Zhang Yuan smiled knowingly.

Longquan Commandery, Huaihuang County. Both were good names.

When locust buds turn yellow, the candidates are busy. Fish and dragons leap in hidden waters, forming patterns.

Before he knew it, dawn was breaking.

On the famous Piyun Mountain, there was a small temple with ordinary incense, called Piyun Temple.

The old temple master, who held several posts at once, was receiving an unfamiliar incense guest surnamed Chen, accompanying him through the main hall, both within the morning mist and clouds.

The old temple master remembered that when he was young, there had also been an incense guest surnamed Cui who asked the same question: why does Heaven’s Dao have no favorites, yet often favor good people.
